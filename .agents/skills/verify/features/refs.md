# Create, rename, delete, and check out branches

## Sub-features

**New branch…** in a commit's menu in the history and in a sidebar branch's menu, **Rename…** and **Delete…** in a local branch's menu, the name dialog with Git's reason under the field, the delete confirmation that says what the branch is not merged into, **New branch from <name>…**, **Rename <name>…**, and **Delete <name>…** in the palette for the sidebar branch last selected, checking out a branch by double click or **Check out** in its menu, **Check out this commit…** with its detach confirmation, **Check out after create** in the new branch dialog, **Check out <name>** in the palette, and undo and redo of each write.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Add the fixture's `repository` path. Right-click the `feat(theme): add the L1 dark canvas` commit, which carries the `v0.1.0` tag, for **New branch…**. Right-click a branch under **Local** or **Remote** in the sidebar, or focus it and press Shift+F10, for the branch items below **Hide** and **Solo**.

## Driving it with T3 preview

Before and after each step, record `git -C <worktree> for-each-ref`, `git -C <worktree> symbolic-ref -q HEAD`, and `git -C <worktree> config --local --get-regexp '^branch\.'` for every worktree you touch. Use a fresh `L1GIT_UNDO_DIR` per run, because the undo history survives restarts.

1. Right-click the theme commit and choose **New branch…**. Confirm the dialog says the branch starts at that commit and focus is in **Branch name**. Type `topic` and press Enter. Confirm `topic` appears under **Local**, its label sits on the theme commit, the commit is selected, and the only change is a new `refs/heads/topic` at that commit.
2. Open **New branch…** again and try `bad..name`, `-x`, `feat/`, and `main`. Confirm each shows Git's reason under the field, such as `'bad..name' is not a valid branch name.` and `A branch named 'main' already exists.`, the dialog stays open, no error notice appears above the history, and nothing changes.
3. Right-click `main` in the sidebar. Confirm **Delete…** is dimmed with `Checked out in l1-demo`. Focus `topic`, press Shift+F10, move to **Rename…**, and press Enter. Confirm the field holds `topic` with all of it selected. Type `work/topic` and press Enter. Confirm the branch moves into a `work` folder, which opens, at the same tip.
4. Run `git -C <repository> branch --set-upstream-to=origin/feat/history feat/squashed`. Choose **Delete…** on `feat/squashed`. Confirm **Cancel** has focus and the dialog reads `feat/squashed is not merged into HEAD (main) or its upstream origin/feat/history.` Click **Delete branch**. Confirm the ref and its `branch.feat/squashed.*` settings are gone and focus moves to the history.
5. Make a branch with three commits only it reaches and an upstream, delete it from its menu, then run `git reflog expire --expire=now --all && git gc --prune=now` and confirm `git cat-file -e <tip>` fails. Click **Undo**. Confirm `git log` reaches all three commits, `git fsck --connectivity-only` passes, the upstream settings are back, and the recordings match the ones before the delete.
6. Create a branch from a remote branch, which sets its upstream, then rename it. Click **Undo** twice and **Redo** twice, running the `reflog expire` and `gc` above before each click. Confirm each undo leaves the recordings identical to the ones before its write and each redo identical to the ones after it.
7. Open the delete dialog on `topic`, run `git branch -f topic HEAD~1` in a terminal, then confirm. Confirm the notice reads `Could not delete branch topic. topic is no longer at the commit L1 Git showed, so nothing changed. Refresh and try again.` and the only change is the terminal's.
8. Add the fixture's `sync.repository`. Open the menu of `develop`, which `sync.dirty` has checked out, and confirm **Delete…** is dimmed with `Checked out in sync-develop`. POST its `deleteBranch` to the bridge and confirm it answers `conflict` and nothing changes.
9. In `sync.repository`, choose **New branch…** on `origin/main` and name it. Confirm the dialog says Git sets its upstream as `git branch` would, the new branch shows its upstream's counts without a reload, and the palette lists **Rename <name>…** and **Delete <name>…** for it.
10. Checking out writes HEAD, the index, and the files, so also record `git -C <worktree> status --porcelain` and `git -C <worktree> ls-files -s` before and after each step below. Click `feat/history` under **Remote** once and confirm it only selects its tip. Then double-click it. Confirm HEAD's label in the history and the green background in the sidebar move to `feat/history`, `symbolic-ref HEAD` reads `refs/heads/feat/history`, a new `refs/heads/feat/history` sits at `origin/feat/history` with `branch.feat/history.remote` and `merge` set, and `ls-files -s` holds that branch's tree.
11. Click **Undo**, then **Redo**. Confirm the undo notice reads `Undid Check out origin/feat/history`, the recordings after undo match the ones before step 10 with the new branch and its settings gone, and the recordings after redo match the ones after step 10.
12. Right-click `main` and confirm **Check out** is dimmed with `Checked out` while HEAD is on `main`. Right-click the theme commit in the history and choose **Check out this commit…**. Confirm the dialog says HEAD will be detached at its short ID and **Cancel** has focus. Press Escape and confirm focus returns to the history. Open it again and click **Check out**. Confirm `symbolic-ref -q HEAD` fails and `rev-parse HEAD` is that commit.
13. Edit a file that both `main` and the target branch hold unchanged, then check out the target. Confirm the edit is still listed under Unstaged and only HEAD and the tree changed. Edit a file the target changes, then check it out. Confirm the notice names that file, no stash appears in `git stash list`, and nothing changed.
14. Open a branch's menu, run `git switch <third branch>` in a terminal without focusing the app, then choose **Check out**. Confirm the notice says HEAD moved since L1 Git read this worktree, the history rereads, and the only change is the terminal's switch.
15. Choose **New branch…** on a commit, type `topic2`, check **Check out after create**, and press Enter. Confirm HEAD is on `topic2`. Press Cmd+Z twice with the history focused. Confirm the first undo puts HEAD back and the second removes `topic2`, and the recordings end identical to the start.
16. Point `core.hooksPath` at a folder whose `post-checkout` prints `hook says no` and exits 1, then check out a branch. Confirm HEAD moved, the notice says the post-checkout hook failed and shows `hook says no`, and **Undo** is offered.
